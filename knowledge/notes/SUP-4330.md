## Problem

AEO asked for four channel-split metrics modelled on the GAP config:
Funded APS U (Web/Store) and APS U (Web/Store), i.e. `ecom_funded_aps_u`,
`store_funded_aps_u`, `ecom_aps_u`, `store_aps_u`. Ticket text told the config
team to copy GAP's `history_metrics_ty.ftl` and `HistoryFit.pivotdefn` pattern,
swap in AEO channel ids, and add the unfunded (non "funded") variants GAP lacks;
viewdefn placement was left to another analyst. Jatin Rana (JIRA 2026-07-14)
challenged the ticket: no PS agreement, "add metrics in all the places" is not a requirement.

## Knowledge gained (techno-functional)

**Technical:** In `UNION ALL` of base tables ClickHouse maps columns by position, so an
out-of-order new field silently crosses metrics. Divide-after-sum: store counts and
net units are summed first, ratio computed in `COMPUTED_METRICS`.
`${ARR_HIST_SC_TABLE}` ARRAY JOIN aliases cannot be JOINed directly in 21.4 and `SELECT *`
through a subquery loses them.

**Functional:** APS = net units per selling store-week; splitting by channel keeps
one eComm location (many units, few "stores") from distorting store APS. Funded APS
only counts weeks where the store is funded.
